## What Can Be Flagged Without the Manual

The following observations are raised as questions that **must be checked against the manual** before any recommendation is issued:

### Humidity Level Concern

The annex has a year-round relative humidity (RH) that **never exceeds 28%**. This is a significant flag:

- Dehumidifiers are typically indicated for environments with **high** humidity (commonly RH ≥ 50–60%).
- At 28% RH, the environment is already quite dry. Operating a dehumidifier at this level risks driving humidity further down, potentially causing:
  - Respiratory discomfort for occupants
  - Damage to timber floors, furniture, and structural joinery
  - Static build-up and damage to electronics

**Before any placement strategy is issued, the manual must be consulted to confirm:**
1. Whether the DryWave 3000 is rated/appropriate for environments with RH ≤ 28%.
2. The minimum RH threshold below which the unit should not be operated.
3. Whether the unit has a humidity target lock or auto-shutoff that prevents over-drying.

### Single-Unit Coverage

The property is one floor with six distinct rooms (kitchen, utility room, bedroom 1, bedroom 2, bathroom, lounge). Key manual lookups required:

- Maximum recommended coverage area (m²) for a single unit.
- Whether the manual recommends a central placement strategy for multi-room coverage.
- Guidance on door/airflow conditions for effective single-unit operation.
- Whether any room types (e.g. bathroom, utility room — likely highest moisture sources) take priority in the manufacturer's placement guidance.
