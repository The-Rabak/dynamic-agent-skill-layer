**Summary/Answer**

The customer's annex presents an unusual situation: a year-round relative humidity (RH) that never exceeds 28%. This reading is extremely low and should be treated with caution. Before deploying a dehumidifier, it is strongly advisable to verify the accuracy of this reading using an external calibrated hygrometer. If the 28% reading is confirmed, continuous dehumidification in this annex is wholly inappropriate. The DryWave 3000 manual is explicit on this point: maintaining humidity below 40% for extended periods in domestic living areas is not recommended, as prolonged low-moisture conditions may lead to human discomfort and certain wood materials contracting or cracking. Furthermore, in conditions of extreme dryness (below 35% RH), the unit should not be operated in Continuous mode, as extended low humidity may cause material dehydration. Operating the dehumidifier continuously in an annex already at 28% RH would risk driving humidity still lower, which could damage wooden floors, fixtures, and furnishings, and create discomfort for occupants.

**Steps/Procedure**

The recommended approach for this annex is as follows:

1. Verify the 28% RH reading with an external calibrated hygrometer before taking any action.
2. If confirmed at or below 28% RH, do not deploy the DryWave 3000 in continuous or sustained extraction mode.
3. If the reading is confirmed, site the single DryWave 3000 unit in the room most susceptible to localised humidity rises — the kitchen, bathroom, or utility/laundry room — as these are areas where cooking, showering, and clothes drying generate bursts of vapour.
4. Set the unit to AUTO mode. AUTO mode is the appropriate setting for general domestic conditioning; it responds dynamically to humidity rises and halts extraction once the target level is restored, avoiding unnecessary over-drying.
5. Set the target RH to 45–50% for normal living spaces. The manual specifies 45–55% as the appropriate range for domestic comfort, with ~45–50% commonly cited for general conditioning.
6. Use the DryWave 3000 only on occasion, to address localised increases in humidity from laundry, cooking, or showering — not as a sustained continuous-extraction device.

**Definitions**

- **Relative Humidity (RH):** A measure of how close the air is to its saturation point at a given temperature, expressed as a percentage.
- **AUTO Mode:** An operating mode in which the DryWave 3000 dynamically adjusts its extraction rate to maintain the user-set target RH, conserving energy by halting when the target is achieved.
- **Continuous Mode:** An operating mode in which the unit runs at maximum extraction capacity until manually disabled or the tank reaches its float-trigger threshold. Not suitable for environments below 35% RH.
