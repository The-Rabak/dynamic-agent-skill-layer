That sounds really tough - starting a new job is stressful enough without worrying about your sleep schedule too! Let me help with your questions.

**Do you need a doctor's note?**

Whether you need a doctor's note when calling in sick really varies by company and manager. Generally speaking, for short absences of just a day or two, most employers don't require documentation. For longer absences, your manager might ask for something. You could check your employee handbook or ask HR directly to get the definitive answer for your specific situation.

**How long to accumulate a week of sick pay?**

It takes time to build up paid sick leave. Typically, companies accrue sick leave over time - often a few hours per month. To get a full week of sick pay (around 40 hours), you'd usually need to be employed for quite a while, often a year or more at many companies. But again, the exact amount depends on your specific company's policy, so I'd recommend checking with HR.

**About sleeping through your alarm:**

Set multiple alarms on your phone as a backup. You might also try moving your phone across the room so you have to physically get up to turn it off. And maybe have a gentle chat with your friend about the importance of getting to sleep at a reasonable hour on weeknights.

Hang in there - the first few weeks of a new job are always the hardest, and it gets easier once you settle in!
